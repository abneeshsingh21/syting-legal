Delete Account & Data

Submit a request to permanently delete your Syting profile, photos, voice recordings, and associated personal records.

Method 1: Instant In-App Deletion (Fastest)

If you currently have the Syting application installed on your device, you can delete your account instantly with zero waiting time:

  1. Open the Syting app on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Navigate to your profile tab and tap the Settings (Gear) icon.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm your choice.

Your account, matches, active chat threads, voice notes, and photos are purged immediately.

Method 2: Web Deletion Request Form

If you have uninstalled the application, lost your mobile device, or cannot log in, submit your registered account details below. Our data compliance team will verify your ownership and purge all records within 48 hours.

⚠️
What happens when your account is deleted?
Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. The following data is permanently purged:
  • Profile details, biography, and age verification record
  • All uploaded profile photos and avatar images
  • Voice intro recordings and private voice notes
  • Chat message history and match connections
  • Location coordinates and notification tokens
