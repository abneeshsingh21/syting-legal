Delete Account & Data

Request deletion of your Syting account and associated personal data, either in the app or by email.

Method 1: In-App Account Deletion

If you currently have the Syting application installed on your device, you can request account deletion directly:

  1. Open the Syting app on your iPhone or Android phone.
  2. Navigate to your profile tab and tap the Settings (Gear) icon.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section at the bottom.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm your choice.

Your account and profile are deleted after confirmation. Media cleanup is retried automatically if storage is temporarily unavailable.

Method 2: Request deletion by email

If you have uninstalled the application, lost your mobile device, or cannot log in, submit your registered account details below. Send a request to hello@asworks.studio from your registered email address. We may need to verify account ownership before processing deletion; do not send passwords or identity documents in this form.

⚠️
What happens when your account is deleted?
Account deletion is permanent and irreversible. It removes your account and associated app data, including:
  • Profile details, biography, and age verification record
  • All uploaded profile photos and avatar images
  • Voice intro recordings and private voice notes
  • Chat message history and match connections
  • Location coordinates and notification tokens

Email hello@asworks.studio with the subject “Syting account deletion request” and your registered account email or phone number. The form below prepares an email; it does not submit data to a server. Send the email yourself to complete the request. No app installation or sign-in is needed to send an email request.
