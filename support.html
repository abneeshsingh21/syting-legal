Frequently Asked Questions

When you match with someone, both of your profile photos start off blurred. As you exchange voice notes, the blur gradually recedes. After both you and your match have sent 3 voice notes each (6 voice notes total), both profile photos are completely revealed! You can also use an optional Instant Unblur pass from Syting Premium if both parties agree.
Syting uses on-device biometric liveness detection and date of birth validation to verify that you are a real person aged 18 or older. The camera check runs 100% locally on your phone—no photos or videos of the verification check are ever transmitted across the internet or stored on our servers. We store only your age range band (e.g. 25–34) and attach an authentic Verified badge to your profile.
Navigate to Settings > Safety > Safe Word in the Syting app. Enter the names and phone numbers of up to 3 trusted personal contacts. You can record a test audio sample and send a test notification to ensure your contacts receive the alert. In a real emergency, triggering the Safe Word captures an audio snippet and immediately broadcasts your approximate location to your trusted contacts.
Subscriptions are managed through your phone's operating system:
  • iPhone / iOS: Open iPhone Settings > tap your name at the top > Subscriptions > Syting > Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Open Google Play Store > tap profile icon > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > Syting > Cancel subscription.
You can delete your account instantly in the app under Settings > Account > Delete account. Alternatively, if you uninstalled the app, you can submit a deletion request through our Web Account Deletion Portal.

Statutory Grievance Redressal Mechanism

In accordance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Syting has appointed a dedicated Resident Grievance Officer for handling user grievances, safety escalations, and regulatory compliance.

⚖️
GRIEVANCE OFFICER DETAILS:
Name: Officer of Grievances, Syting Operations
Designation: Chief Grievance & Compliance Officer
Office Address: Syting Technology Pvt. Ltd., Level 4, DLF Cyber City, New Delhi, NCR, India
Official Grievance Email: grievance@syting.app

Statutory Grievance Redressal Timelines:

  • Acknowledgment: All grievance complaints received via the official grievance email will be acknowledged with a unique tracking ticket within 24 hours of receipt.
  • Resolution: The Grievance Officer will dispose of the grievance and communicate the final resolution to the complainant within 15 calendar days from the date of receipt.
  • Emergency Escalations: Complaints regarding non-consensual sexually explicit content, impersonation, or emergency safety concerns are prioritized and resolved within 24 hours.
