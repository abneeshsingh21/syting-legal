Privacy Policy

How Syting collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal information.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: October 25, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At Syting ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy, safety, and personal dignity are fundamental to everything we build. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your personal information across the Syting mobile application on iOS and Android. Your privacy rights depend on your jurisdiction and applicable law. Contact hello@asworks.studio with questions or requests about your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide you with secure, authentic, and high-quality dating experiences:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Registration: Your name, email address, phone number (if used for authentication), age band, gender identity, and matching preferences. Your declared birth date is used during onboarding to assess age; your profile stores an age band, not the exact birth date.
  • Profile Information: Profile photographs, biography, lifestyle interests, and your short voice intro recording.
  • Communications: Private messages, voice notes exchanged with matched partners, and feedback ratings submitted after dates.

B. Self-Declared Age & Camera Checks

Syting is for adults aged 18 and over. During onboarding, we use your declared date of birth and camera checks. These checks are trust signals; they do not establish government-verified identity or guarantee a person's age.

🛡️
HOW CAMERA INFORMATION IS PROCESSED:
Camera checks include on-device processing. A captured selfie and claimed age can also be sent through our server to Google Gemini for an anti-spoofing and adult-age plausibility assessment. The verification endpoint stores the age band, method, status, and timestamps rather than saving the raw capture to app storage. Provider processing is subject to its service terms. Aadhaar and DigiLocker verification are disabled in this release.

C. Location Information

  • Discovery Distance: With your permission, Syting reads your device location and sends a coarse geohash to support nearby discovery. Your exact GPS coordinates are never made visible to other users.
  • Safe Word: When you explicitly trigger Safe Word and grant permissions, a location message can be opened in your phone's SMS composer for chosen trusted contacts. You control sending that message. Optional microphone audio is saved in private app storage for safety evidence. This feature does not automatically dispatch emergency services.

D. Video Dates (LiveKit WebRTC)

Syting uses LiveKit infrastructure to transport live audio and video over encrypted WebRTC connections. The calling flow does not create call recordings. A separately activated Safe Word recording can capture microphone audio. Call invitations and call-history metadata are stored to support calling and delivery.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate, authorized purposes:

  • Operating our bidirectional matchmaking discovery algorithm based on your gender, age, and location preferences.
  • Powering our signature "3+3 Voice Note Reveal" progression, gradually unblurring photos as voice notes are exchanged.
  • Enforcing community safety, detecting fraudulent or underage accounts, and calculating Aura reputation scores.
  • Processing in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple App Store and Google Play Billing.
  • Providing customer support and investigating reports of abuse or harassment.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-processors

We use service providers to operate accounts, communications, billing, diagnostics, and camera verification:

  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud hosting, encrypted database (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security), secure user authentication, and encrypted media storage.
  • LiveKit Cloud: Real-time encrypted WebRTC media transport infrastructure for video calls.
  • RevenueCat Inc.: In-app purchase receipt validation and subscription entitlement management.
  • Firebase: Push notifications and crash diagnostics, which can include installation identifiers and technical device information.
  • Mixpanel: Optional product analytics use a pseudonymous app account identifier and restricted event properties. Analytics starts only after opt-in. Conversation text, profile names, contact details, precise location, and arbitrary nested properties are excluded from analytics events.
  • Google Gemini: Camera and profile-content safety assessments. AI Cupid initially offers general starters without sending chat context to a model. If you choose “Generate with chat context,” up to 10 recent messages and approved profile bio/interests can be shared for suggestions. Some identifiers are removed, but other personal details may remain. You review and send suggestions yourself.
  • Apple & Google: Platform-level authentication, app installation, and in-app payment processing.

We never sell your personal information or profile data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

We retain account data while needed to provide the service and handle applicable security, legal and deletion obligations. You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time in the app by visiting Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Chat Attachments: A scheduled cleanup removes chat-media objects after seven days and replaces affected message content with expiration markers. Safety audio has a 30-day cleanup period.
  • Local Offline Chat, Where Enabled: Outgoing messages, owned attachments, drafts, and recent chat snapshots use encrypted, account-scoped device storage. Pending messages and drafts are available for up to seven days; expired entries are removed when the account's store next opens or retries. Signing into another account does not expose them. Server acceptance removes queued attachment copies. Device encryption does not make server-accessible chats end-to-end encrypted.
  • Deletion Without the App: Visit our account-deletion page for instructions, or email hello@asworks.studio from your registered address. The page prepares an email; you must send it yourself. Account ownership may need to be verified.
  • Deletion Scope: The in-app flow removes your account and associated profile records, photos, voice introductions, chat media and app records. Email requests are processed after ownership verification. Data held by external service providers, backups and any legally required retention can follow separate schedules. Contact us for details; a web form alone does not delete your account.

6. Security Safeguards

We employ enterprise-grade security controls to protect your data:

  • The app uses HTTPS for backend requests and encrypted WebRTC transport for calls. This does not mean stored messages are end-to-end encrypted.
  • Database records are protected by granular PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, preventing unauthorized read or write access.
  • Profile photos and audio notes are stored in secure object buckets protected by time-limited signed access URLs.

7. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you hold specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit and update your profile information at any time within the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You can request account deletion in the app or by emailing hello@asworks.studio. Account ownership may need to be verified before an email request is processed; retention required by applicable law may still apply.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke location or camera permissions at any time via your device operating system settings.
