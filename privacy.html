Privacy Policy

How Syting safeguards your personal information, protects your privacy, and guarantees on-device security.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Last Revised: September 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

At Syting ("we," "our," or "us"), your privacy, safety, and personal dignity are fundamental to everything we build. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your personal information across the Syting mobile application on iOS and Android. This policy complies with international data privacy standards, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide you with secure, authentic, and high-quality dating experiences:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Registration: Your name, email address, phone number (if used for authentication), date of birth, gender identity, and matching preferences.
  • Profile Information: Profile photographs, biography, lifestyle interests, and your short voice intro recording.
  • Communications: Private messages, voice notes exchanged with matched partners, and feedback ratings submitted after dates.

B. Age & Identity Verification Information (On-Device Privacy Guarantee)

To eliminate automated bots and ensure our strict 18+ community policy is enforced, Syting employs on-device biometric selfie liveness verification and date of birth validation during onboarding:

🛡️
OUR ZERO-DOCUMENT & ON-DEVICE PRIVACY GUARANTEE:
Syting DOES NOT harvest, sell, or permanently store raw biometric videos or sensitive identity documents. The liveness check runs entirely within your device's local memory to confirm you are a real human. We store only your age range band (e.g. 24–30) and a verification status badge (age_verified = true). Where digital or government-backed credential checks may be introduced, they operate strictly on a zero-knowledge confirmation basis without retaining raw identity paperwork.

C. Location Information

  • Discovery Distance: With your permission, Syting collects coarse location coordinates to calculate mutual distance between prospective matches. Your exact GPS coordinates are never made visible to other users.
  • Safe Word Emergency Broadcast: Real-time precise GPS coordinates and emergency audio are shared only if you explicitly trigger the Safe Word panic feature, and are transmitted strictly to your pre-configured personal trusted contacts.

D. Video Dates (LiveKit WebRTC)

Syting enables real-time video dates powered by LiveKit Cloud WebRTC technology. Video and audio streams are transmitted peer-to-peer over encrypted channels in real-time. Syting does not record, archive, or save video dates. Streams are completely ephemeral and cease existing the moment the call terminates.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate, authorized purposes:

  • Operating our bidirectional matchmaking discovery algorithm based on your gender, age, and location preferences.
  • Powering our signature "3+3 Voice Note Reveal" progression, gradually unblurring photos as voice notes are exchanged.
  • Enforcing community safety, detecting fraudulent or underage accounts, and calculating Aura reputation scores.
  • Processing in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple App Store and Google Play Billing.
  • Providing customer support and investigating reports of abuse or harassment.

4. Third-Party Service Providers & Sub-processors

We partner with vetted, industry-leading infrastructure providers to ensure high security and reliability. These service providers act strictly as data processors under confidentiality agreements:

  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud hosting, encrypted database (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security), secure user authentication, and encrypted media storage.
  • LiveKit Cloud: Real-time encrypted WebRTC media transport infrastructure for video calls.
  • RevenueCat Inc.: In-app purchase receipt validation and subscription entitlement management.
  • Firebase / Mixpanel: Anonymous crash diagnostics, product performance metrics, and push notifications.
  • Apple & Google: Platform-level authentication, app installation, and in-app payment processing.

We never sell your personal information or profile data to third-party data brokers, marketers, or advertisers.

5. Data Retention & Permanent Account Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as your account remains active. You have full ownership and control over your data:

  • Instant In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time in the app by visiting Settings > Account > Delete account.
  • Web-Based Deletion Portal: If you have uninstalled the app or lost device access, you can submit a deletion request via our web portal at syting.app/delete-account.
  • Permanent Purge: Upon account deletion, all personal data—including your profile pictures, voice intro recordings, voice notes, chat logs, match history, and location data—is permanently deleted from our primary database and storage buckets within 30 days, except where retention is strictly required by statutory law (e.g. anti-fraud or financial records).

6. Security Safeguards

We employ enterprise-grade security controls to protect your data:

  • All network communications are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3).
  • Database records are protected by granular PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, preventing unauthorized read or write access.
  • Profile photos and audio notes are stored in secure object buckets protected by time-limited signed access URLs.

7. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR, CCPA & India DPDP Act)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you hold specific statutory privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can edit and update your profile information at any time within the app settings.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You have the unconditional right to have your account and all associated personal data erased permanently.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You may revoke location or camera permissions at any time via your device operating system settings.
