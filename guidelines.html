The Syting Code

Community Guidelines

Our shared standards for building safe, authentic, and emotionally rewarding connections.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. The Four Pillars of Syting

Syting was designed to bring intention and authenticity back to modern dating. When you step into our community, you agree to honor four fundamental pillars:

  1. Authenticity Over Superficiality: Present your real self. Use genuine photos, record your own voice intro, and complete age verification. We believe who you are on the inside matters just as much as how you look.
  2. Mutual Respect & Clear Consent: Every interaction must be consensual. No pressure, no uninvited sexual commentary, and no boundary violations. Treat every match with the empathy you would expect in person.
  3. Respect the Reveal: The "3+3 Voice Note Reveal" was created to allow personality and voice to spark genuine curiosity before looks take center stage. Respect your match's pace as the photos unblur.
  4. Proactive Safety: Look out for yourself and your peers. Report red flags, maintain healthy boundaries, and use our built-in safety tools.

2. What Is Strictly Prohibited

To ensure a safe environment for everyone, we enforce a zero-tolerance policy against the following behaviors:

  • Harassment & Hate Speech: Bullying, intimidation, slurs, discrimination, misogyny, body-shaming, or stalking will result in an immediate and irreversible ban.
  • Unsolicited Explicit Media: Sending unrequested sexual photos, lewd voice notes, or indecent video during calls is strictly forbidden.
  • Catfishing & Impersonation: Using someone else's photos, misrepresenting your age or identity, or using AI-generated avatars to deceive matches is strictly prohibited.
  • Financial Fraud & Commercial Solicitation: Never solicit money, cryptocurrency, loans, gifts, or promote commercial services, escorting, or social media handles for follower gains.
  • Underage Participation: Syting is exclusively 18+. Any attempt by minors to join or individuals attempting to solicit minors will result in immediate law enforcement referral.

3. The Aura Reputation System

Syting features a dynamic Aura Score that rewards respectful dating etiquette and disincentivizes toxic behavior:

  • Boosting Your Aura: Answering messages within 24 hours (+10), completing respectful video dates (+20), receiving positive post-date feedback (+15), and maintaining verified respectful interactions (+25).
  • Damaging Your Aura: Repeated ghosting without courtesy (-30), verified harassment reports (-50), or disrespectful behavior in calls. Profiles with severely low Aura scores lose visibility and face automatic suspension.

4. Real-World & In-Person Dating Safety Tips

When you decide to take your Syting connection into the real world, always prioritize your physical safety:

🛡️
SYTING SAFETY CHECKLIST:
  • Do a Video Date First: Spend 5 minutes on a Syting LiveKit video date before agreeing to an in-person meetup to confirm identity and chemistry.
  • Meet in a Busy Public Place: Always schedule first dates in public venues (cafes, restaurants, parks) with plenty of people around.
  • Tell a Friend: Inform a trusted friend or family member where you are going, who you are meeting, and what time you expect to return.
  • Arrange Your Own Transport: Always drive yourself, take public transit, or use a rideshare service. Never let a new date pick you up from your residence.
  • Configure Your Safe Word: Go to Settings > Safety > Safe Word in Syting and designate your emergency contacts before going on a date.

5. How to Report & Block

If anyone makes you feel uncomfortable, pressured, or unsafe, do not hesitate to act:

  • Block: Tap the options menu (three dots) on any profile or chat and select Block. They will immediately disappear from your matches.
  • Report: Select Report User, pick the reason (e.g. harassment, impersonation, inappropriate photos), and provide any details.
  • Our 24-Hour Review Guarantee: Our safety team investigates 100% of reports within 24 hours. Offending accounts are dealt with decisively.

6. Community Questions

Have questions about community standards or want to appeal a moderation decision? Contact our team at community@syting.app.
